Frequently Asked Questions Quick answers about eSIMs, coverage, and how revenue sharing works
Is RoamFi legit? Yes — RoamFi operates in 200+ destinations with transparent revenue-sharing payouts and standard eSIM connectivity through licensed local carriers.
How is RoamFi different from Airalo or Holafly? Same global eSIM coverage, plus business revenue sharing: every purchase you make also earns you a share of future sales for 365 days — something other eSIMs don't offer.
Do I need to refer people to earn? No. Simply buying an eSIM automatically enrolls you in the revenue-sharing pool. If anyone in the world buys a RoamFi eSIM after you, you earn a share of that purchase.

How money flows
You purchased eSIM
Someone purchased eSIM after you
Example John buys a RoamFi eSIM (United States, 10GB / 30 days) for $20. RoamFi pays the eSIM provider (a local third-party company in the United States) $10 cost price and keeps $10 as business revenue. RoamFi then keeps $5 as company revenue, while allocating the other $5 to the revenue sharing pool:
— $2 to pool A (40%) — $1.5 to pool B (30%) — $1 to pool C (20%) — $0.5 to pool D (10%)
All users who purchased any RoamFi eSIM before John will get a share of USD from the revenue sharing pools, based on their position among these pools.

Revenue sharing pool
Every eSIM purchase you make becomes a spot in the revenue sharing pool with a certain weight. Users who purchase eSIMs and generate business revenue for RoamFi after you keep topping up the revenue sharing pool, and your USD earnings are distributed from it in an automatic real-time settlement. Users in the same pool share USD earnings proportionally to their eSIM purchase amount.
